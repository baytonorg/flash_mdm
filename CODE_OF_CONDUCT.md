# Code of Conduct

We want Flash MDM to be a respectful, constructive project for users,
contributors, and maintainers. Be considerate, communicate in good faith, and
focus criticism on ideas and implementation rather than people.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained
disruption, or publishing another person's private information without permission.

Report concerns privately to [jason@bayton.org](mailto:jason@bayton.org). The
maintainer may remove content, limit participation, or take other appropriate
action to protect the community.
